Can DOOM run Smash&Clash?

The 1993 engine draws the board, the cards and the menus, and plays online against any Smash&Clash client.

Play

  • Walk up to any computer screen and press Space or E.
  • Or press C anywhere, or pick Smash&Clash in DOOM's main menu.
  • In the terminal: arrows and Enter, or the mouse. 1–5 pick a card, Tab lists every legal move, Esc goes back to DOOM.

DOOM

  • WASD or arrows to move, Ctrl to fire.
  • Click the screen to aim with the mouse.

Smash&Clash, its characters, art and audio are Smash&Clash's, used as its client terms allow. Built on @smashandclash/sdk (MIT). DOOM engine: doomgeneric (GPL-2.0). Game data: Freedoom (BSD).
